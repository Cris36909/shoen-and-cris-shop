About Shoen & Cris

Shoen & Cris: Plug & Play is a computer hardware and electronics store built for PC builders, gamers, and tech enthusiasts.

Our Mission

To make quality computer hardware accessible and understandable for everyone — from first-time builders to seasoned enthusiasts. We believe in transparent pricing, honest guidance, and dependable components.

Our Vision

To be the go-to destination for PC building and electronics in the Philippines, known for curated selections, fair prices, and genuine support for the community.

What We Specialize In

  • Custom PC builds for gaming, productivity, and workstations
  • Computer hardware: GPUs, CPUs, motherboards, RAM, storage
  • Gaming peripherals and accessories
  • Monitors, cables, and electronic gadgets
  • Smartphones, tablets, smartwatches, and cameras

Why Choose Us

  • Curated catalog with realistic product specifications
  • Clear pricing in Philippine Peso
  • Functional filtering, search, and sorting
  • Wishlist and cart persistence
  • Simulated checkout and order history
